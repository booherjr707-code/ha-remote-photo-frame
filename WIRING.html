Photo Frame Remote Wiring

The display, its touch chip and its SD card slot all share three lines: clock (SCK), data out (MOSI) and data in (MISO). So ESP32 pins D18, D23 and D19 each get several wires. Use a breadboard row for each of those three. Tick each wire off as you go. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

ESP32-WROOM-32 dev board Hosyond 4.0" 480×320 SPI touch display microSD card, 32 GB or smaller Breadboard + 16 jumper wires

Leave SDO (MISO) on the main row empty. On many of these 4" boards the screen's SDO pin doesn't let go of the line and garbles the touch and SD card readings. The screen doesn't need it. Only T_DO and SD_MISO go to D19. The SD slot's 4 pins are on the other end of the board from the main row.

First power-up: what you should see

  1. "Touch calibration", then an arrow in each corner. Tap each one. This only happens the first time; hold a finger on the screen while it starts to do it again.
  2. "Connecting to Wi-Fi", then the Weather page with tabs along the bottom: Weather, Driveway, Controls.
  3. Until the Home Assistant token is added, the pages say "Home Assistant isn't set up yet". That's expected. The dot in the top-right corner turns green once Home Assistant answers.
  4. After 5 minutes without a touch, the picture frame starts. Tap anywhere to come back.
  5. Blank white screen: check CS, DC and RESET. Taps in the wrong spot: recalibrate. "No SD card" in the frame: check the four SD wires and that the card is FAT32.
